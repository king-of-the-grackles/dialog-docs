Dialog can deliver research results directly to your Telegram account. This is useful for scheduled research or when you want to receive findings without opening the app.

## Setting up Telegram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** in the Dialog app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Telegram">
    In the integrations section, click **Connect Telegram**. Follow the prompts to link your Telegram account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    Dialog will send a test message to your Telegram chat to confirm the connection is working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How delivery works

Once connected, Dialog can push research results to your Telegram chat:

- Results are formatted for readability in Telegram's interface
- Long research briefs are automatically split into multiple messages
- Links and citations are preserved as clickable Telegram links

    Get notified when there's new discussion about your competitors
  </Card>

    Kick off research in the app, close it, and get results in Telegram later

## Disconnecting Telegram

To disconnect your Telegram account, go to **Settings** and click **Disconnect** next to the Telegram integration. Your research history in the app is unaffected.
