Dialog uses a credit-based billing system. Each research query consumes credits based on the complexity and data sources used.

## How credits work

- Each research query uses a variable number of credits depending on:
  - The complexity of the question
  - How many data sources are consulted
  - The length of the AI analysis
- Your current credit balance is displayed in the app header
- You'll receive a warning when your balance is running low

## Checking your balance

Your credit balance is always visible in the top navigation bar. For detailed usage information, go to **Settings** to see:

- Current credit balance
- Subscription tier
- Usage history
- Billing details

## Managing your subscription

Dialog integrates with Stripe for payment processing. From Settings, you can:

- **View your current plan** and usage
- **Manage your subscription** through the Stripe Customer Portal
- **Update payment methods** and billing information
- **View invoices** and payment history

<Info>
Click **Manage Subscription** in Settings to open the Stripe Customer Portal, where you can update your plan, payment method, and billing details.
</Info>

## Need more credits?

If you're running low on credits or need a higher tier, visit the subscription management page in Settings to upgrade your plan.
