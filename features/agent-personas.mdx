# Agent Personas

Dialog offers specialized AI agent personas, each optimized for a different type of research task. Think of them as team members with distinct expertise.

## Available personas

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chief of Staff" icon="briefcase">
    Strategic research assistant. Excels at synthesizing information across sources, identifying priorities, and producing executive-level briefs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Research Analyst" icon="chart-mixed">
    Deep-dive researcher. Goes thoroughly into topics, pulls extensive data, and produces detailed analysis with comprehensive citations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content Strategist" icon="pen-nib">
    Content-focused research. Identifies trending topics, analyzes what resonates with audiences, and surfaces content opportunities.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom Agent" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Build your own agent persona with a custom name and specialized instructions tailored to your specific research needs.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Choosing a persona

Each persona brings a different lens to your research:

| Persona | Best for | Output style |
|---|---|---|
| Chief of Staff | Strategic decisions, competitive landscape | Executive briefs, prioritized insights |
| Research Analyst | Deep market research, trend analysis | Detailed reports, data-heavy findings |
| Content Strategist | Content planning, audience understanding | Topic suggestions, engagement analysis |
| Custom | Your specific use case | Defined by your instructions |

## Switching personas

You can switch between agent personas from the chat interface. Each persona maintains its own approach to researching and structuring responses, so experiment to find which works best for your use case.

<Tip>
Start with the **Research Analyst** persona for your first few queries. It provides the most comprehensive output and helps you understand the depth of data Dialog can access.
</Tip>
