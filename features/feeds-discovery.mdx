Enter any website URL and Dialog analyzes it to find the Reddit communities most relevant to your audience — no manual searching required.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your website URL">
    Paste any URL into the discovery tool. This can be your own site, a competitor's page, or a product in your space. Dialog visits the page and analyzes its content.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the analysis">
    Dialog extracts a 2-3 sentence site description, identifies 3 or more audience personas, and generates 12-18 targeted keywords. Results stream in real time so you can watch the analysis unfold.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select subreddits">
    Based on the analysis, Dialog recommends subreddits ranked by relevance and community size. Mega-subreddits like r/all and r/popular are filtered out automatically so you get focused, high-signal communities.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your feed">
    Pick the subreddits that match your goals and save them as a curated [feed](/features/reddit-feeds) for ongoing monitoring.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the analysis includes

Each URL analysis produces three components that drive subreddit recommendations:

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Site description** | A 2-3 sentence summary of what the site does and who it serves |
| **Audience personas** | 3+ distinct user profiles who would visit the site |
| **Keywords** | 12-18 terms covering topics, products, and pain points |

These components work together to match your site against Reddit communities where your audience is already active.

## Better results, fewer distractions

Dialog's recommendations are designed to surface communities where real conversations happen. The discovery engine automatically filters out oversized, general-purpose subreddits that add noise without value.

<Tip>
Run discovery against multiple URLs to compare results. Try your homepage, a competitor's landing page, and a relevant blog post to build a well-rounded feed.
</Tip>

## From discovery to monitoring

Once you create a feed from your discovery results, it appears in your sidebar for quick access. You can continue adding subreddits manually or run discovery again with a different URL.

For details on browsing and managing your feeds, see [Reddit Feeds](/features/reddit-feeds) and [Managing Feeds](/guides/managing-feeds).
