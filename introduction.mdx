## Why Dialog?

Product teams are drowning in scattered signals -- competitor launches on one platform, customer complaints on another, trending conversations buried in threads nobody has time to read. Dialog gives you a parallel team of AI agents to handle it.

- **Start with a Chief of Staff, add more as you grow** -- Every account begins with a default Chief of Staff agent. Add specialized agents -- for content, analytics, research, or any custom persona -- as your needs expand.
- **74+ tools connected** -- Research across Reddit, social media, web, and your existing stack (Gmail, Slack, Jira, Linear, Notion, and more)
- **Persistent workspace** -- Every link saved, every research note, every task tracked across sessions. Your agents remember everything.
- **Delivered where you work** -- Get briefings and results via Telegram or Slack today. Discord and WhatsApp are coming soon.

## What people use Dialog for

- **Saving and organizing** -- Send links, articles, and resources to your agent. It scrapes, enriches, tags, and files them for later.
- **Competitive intelligence** -- Monitor competitor positioning, pricing changes, and product launches across channels
- **Content creation** -- Ideate LinkedIn posts, draft marketing copy, and iterate on messaging with your agent
- **Task management** -- Track to-dos, project milestones, and action items conversationally
- **Research** -- Deep-dive any topic across Reddit, social media, and the web with sourced citations
- **Scheduled briefings** -- Set up daily or weekly monitoring and get results pushed to you automatically

## Who is Dialog for?

- **Founders** validating ideas, tracking competitors, and managing their growing to-do list
- **Product managers** monitoring competitive landscape, synthesizing user feedback, and building research briefs
- **Growth teams** discovering customer conversations, emerging trends, and content opportunities
- **Solo operators** who need a Chief of Staff that never sleeps
