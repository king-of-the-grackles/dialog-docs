## Why Dialog?

Product teams spend hours every week manually scanning Reddit threads, competitor forums, and community discussions for signal. Dialog automates that entire workflow:

- **Ask anything** - Type a natural language question and get a structured research brief
- **Multi-source intelligence** - Pull insights from Reddit, web scraping, social media, and more
- **Persistent memory** - Your research history is saved and searchable across devices
- **Delivered where you work** - Get results in-app or pushed to Telegram

## Who is Dialog for?

- **Founders** validating ideas and tracking market sentiment
- **Product managers** monitoring competitive landscape and user feedback
- **Growth teams** discovering customer conversations and emerging trends
- **Indie hackers** finding potential customers and understanding pain points
