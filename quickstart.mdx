Follow these steps to go from sign-up to your first research insight.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [app.dialog.tools](https://app.dialog.tools) and sign up with your email. You can use a password or magic link to authenticate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    Once you're in the app, you'll see the research chat. Type a natural language question like:

    ```
    What are the biggest pain points people have with project management tools?
    ```

    Dialog will search across Reddit communities, analyze discussions, and synthesize the findings into a structured brief.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review your results">
    Dialog streams results in real time. You'll see:
    - **Thinking indicators** showing the AI's reasoning process
    - **Source citations** linking back to original Reddit posts and comments
    - **Structured findings** organized by theme or sentiment

    Your research is automatically saved to your session history.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore further">
    From here you can:
    - Ask follow-up questions in the same session
    - [Create a Reddit feed](/features/reddit-feeds) to monitor specific communities
    - [Connect Telegram](/guides/telegram-delivery) for push delivery
    - Browse your past sessions in the sidebar
  </Step>
</Steps>
