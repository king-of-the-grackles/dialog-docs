Getting started with Dialog takes less than a minute.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit Dialog">
    Go to [app.dialog.tools](https://app.dialog.tools) and click **Get Started**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your sign-up method">
    You can create an account using:
    - **Magic link** - Receive a one-click login link via email
    - **GitHub** - Sign in with your GitHub account
    - **LinkedIn** - Sign in with your LinkedIn account
    - **Google** - Sign in with your Google account
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email and confirm your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start researching">
    Once verified, you'll land in your research workspace ready to ask your first question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your workspace

After signing up, you get a personal workspace that includes:

- **Research chat** - The main interface for asking questions
- **Session sidebar** - Browse and switch between past research sessions
- **Reddit feeds** - Create and manage curated community feeds
- **Settings** - Manage your account, billing, and integrations

## Signing in on other devices

Dialog syncs your research across devices. Sign in with the same email on any browser to access your full session history and feeds.
