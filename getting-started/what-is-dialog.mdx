Dialog is an AI agent platform that gives product teams a parallel workforce of specialized assistants. Each agent has its own persistent workspace, access to 74+ tools, and memory that builds over time. Think of it as deploying a team that researches, monitors, saves, drafts, and organizes on your behalf.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy your agents">
    Every account starts with a default agent ready to go. As your needs grow, create specialized agents -- a Research Analyst for competitive intel, a Content Strategist for LinkedIn, a custom persona for whatever you need.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Talk to them naturally">
    Send messages the way you'd talk to a colleague. Save a link, ask a research question, add a task, or request a content draft. Your agent figures out which tools to use and handles the rest.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Agents build a knowledge base">
    Every interaction builds on the last. Links are scraped and filed. Research notes accumulate. Tasks are tracked. Your agent's workspace becomes a living knowledge base you can reference anytime.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get results where you work">
    View results in real time via streaming in the app, or receive briefings through Telegram, Slack, Discord, or WhatsApp. Set up scheduled tasks for automated daily or weekly monitoring.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Named agent personas** | Deploy specialized agents like Chief of Staff, Research Analyst, or Content Strategist |
| **74+ connected tools** | Reddit, social media, web search, scraping, and connected apps like Gmail, Jira, Slack, Notion |
| **Persistent workspace** | Files, research notes, tasks, databases, and bookmarks that persist across every session |
| **Link saving & enrichment** | Send any URL and your agent scrapes, summarizes, tags, and files it |
| **Content creation** | Ideate, draft, and iterate on posts, copy, and marketing content |
| **Task management** | Conversational to-do tracking with TASKS.md |
| **Scheduled automation** | Set up recurring research, monitoring, and briefings |
| **Multi-channel delivery** | Results delivered via app, Telegram, Slack, Discord, or WhatsApp |
| **Custom skills** | Teach your agent reusable workflows tailored to how you work |

## What makes Dialog different

- **Agents, not just answers** -- Dialog isn't a search engine. Your agents persist across sessions, accumulate context, and get better the more you use them.
- **Real sources, not hallucinations** -- Every research insight links back to actual posts, comments, and web pages. Verify anything with one click.
- **Your knowledge base grows automatically** -- Every link saved, every research brief, every competitive scan gets filed in your workspace. Nothing is lost.
- **74+ tools, zero configuration** -- Your agents automatically select the right tools for each task. Reddit, Instagram, web scraping, Gmail, Jira -- it all works out of the box.
- **Works where you do** -- Chat in the app, get briefings on Telegram, or trigger workflows from Slack. Your agents are always reachable.
