# What is Dialog?

Dialog is an AI research assistant that connects to multiple data sources through the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to answer your questions with real, sourced intelligence. Think of it as having a research analyst on your team who can scan thousands of online discussions in seconds.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You ask a question">
    Type any research question in natural language. Dialog understands context, so you can be specific or broad.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dialog searches multiple sources">
    Behind the scenes, Dialog connects to Reddit communities, web sources, and other data providers to gather relevant discussions, posts, and content.
  </Step>

  <Step title="AI synthesizes the findings">
    Claude AI analyzes the raw data, identifies patterns, and produces a structured research brief with citations back to the original sources.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Results are delivered to you">
    View results in real time via streaming, or receive them later through Telegram. All research is saved to your session history for future reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Natural language queries** | Ask questions the way you'd ask a colleague |
| **Multi-source research** | Reddit, web scraping, social media, and more |
| **Real-time streaming** | Watch results appear as they're generated |
| **Session history** | All research saved and searchable across devices |
| **Reddit feeds** | Create curated feeds to monitor specific communities |
| **Telegram delivery** | Get research pushed to your Telegram chat |
| **Agent personas** | Specialized AI agents for different research tasks |

## What makes Dialog different

- **Purpose-built for research** - Not a generic chatbot. Dialog is designed specifically for market research, competitive intelligence, and customer discovery.
- **Real sources, not hallucinations** - Every insight links back to actual Reddit posts, comments, and web pages.
- **Persistent across sessions** - Your research history follows you across devices, so you never lose context.
- **Multiple data sources** - Goes beyond a single platform to pull from Reddit, the web, and other community sources.
