Dialog offers specialized AI agent personas, each optimized for a different type of work. Think of them as team members with distinct expertise and working styles.

<Info>
Personas are pre-configured agent profiles. To learn about the underlying agent system, see [What is an Agent?](/core-concepts/agents/what-is-an-agent) and [Creating Agents](/core-concepts/agents/creating-agents).
</Info>

## Choosing a persona

Each persona brings a different lens to your work:

| Persona | Best for | Typical tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Chief of Staff | Day-to-day operations, personal productivity | Save links, manage tasks, daily recaps, organize workspace, track projects |
| Research Analyst | Competitive intelligence, market research | Competitive reports, trend analysis, market sizing, comprehensive research briefs |
| Content Strategist | Content creation, marketing | Draft posts, workshop copy, content ideation, audience analysis, pipeline management |
| Custom | Your specific workflow | Defined by your instructions |

## Persona archetypes to consider

Beyond the built-in personas, here are agent configurations that work well for specific roles:

| Archetype | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Competitor Tracker** | Monitor competitor launches, pricing changes, and positioning updates |
| **Growth Agent** | Lead qualification, outreach research, and partnership signals |
| **Analytics Agent** | KPI summaries, anomaly detection, and performance metrics |
| **Community Intel** | Reddit, Hacker News, and X conversation monitoring |
| **Feedback Synthesizer** | Cluster user feedback into themes from support tickets and forums |
| **DevRel Agent** | GitHub issues, developer pain points, and technical community monitoring |

You can create any of these by making a [custom agent](/core-concepts/agents/creating-agents) with the right name, purpose, and instructions.

## How personas relate to agents

Each persona is backed by a full [agent](/core-concepts/agents/what-is-an-agent) with its own workspace and memory. When you switch personas, you're switching to a different agent -- each with its own conversation history, saved research, task list, and custom skills.

This means your Chief of Staff's saved links and task list stay separate from your Research Analyst's competitive reports. Each persona operates in its own isolated workspace.

This separation is powerful for keeping different contexts clean. Your competitive intelligence stays with the Research Analyst. Your content pipeline stays with the Content Strategist. Your daily operations stay with your Chief of Staff.

## Switching personas

You can switch between agent personas from the chat interface. Each persona maintains its own approach to tasks, so experiment to find which works best for each workflow.

<Tip>
Start with the **Chief of Staff** persona for general use. It handles the broadest range of tasks -- saving links, managing tasks, research, and organizing your workspace. Create specialized agents as your needs grow.
</Tip>

You can also [create your own agents](/core-concepts/agents/creating-agents) with custom personas -- combining the personality and instructions you need with the data sources that matter most.
