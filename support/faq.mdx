<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What data sources does Dialog use?">
    Dialog primarily uses Reddit as its data source, with additional capabilities for web search and scraping. The platform connects to multiple data providers through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), allowing it to pull from Reddit communities, web pages, and other sources.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my research data private?">
    Yes. Your research sessions, feeds, and account data are private to your account. Sessions are stored securely and only accessible when you're authenticated.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share my research with others?">
    Yes. Dialog supports session sharing, which generates a shareable link that others can view without needing an account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How accurate are the results?">
    Dialog's results are grounded in real data from Reddit posts and comments. Every insight includes citations linking back to the original source material so you can verify the findings yourself. The AI synthesizes and summarizes the data but always references its sources.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my query take a long time?">
    Query time depends on several factors: the breadth of your question, how many communities need to be searched, and the volume of data being analyzed. More specific queries tend to return faster. Very broad questions may take longer as Dialog searches across more sources.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Dialog on mobile?">
    Yes. Dialog is a web application that works in any modern browser, including mobile browsers. Your sessions sync across all devices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    When your credit balance reaches zero, you won't be able to submit new research queries. You can still browse your existing sessions and feeds. Visit Settings to upgrade your plan or purchase more credits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Contact support to request account deletion. All your sessions, feeds, and personal data will be permanently removed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my research?">
    Research sessions are displayed in rich text format that you can copy and paste into your preferred tools. Direct export features are on the roadmap.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
